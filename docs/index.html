UnfoldAI

Build the skills that move your life forward.

UnfoldAI is a personal AI mentor for real-life skills such as communication, confidence, discipline, problem-solving and adaptability. It understands your situation, gives you one practical task at a time, waits for you to try it, analyses how it went, and tracks your progress only when there is real evidence.

How it works

  1. Sign in with Google or email and rate your five core skills.
  2. Pick a skill and tell your AI coach what is going on.
  3. Get one personalised, real-world task for today.
  4. Try it, then tell your coach how it went.
  5. Receive an honest analysis and your next task.
