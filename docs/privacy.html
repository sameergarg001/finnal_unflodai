Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

What we collect

  • Account information: your email address, and if you sign in with Google, your name, Google account ID and profile picture. If you sign up with email, a securely hashed password.
  • Profile information you choose to give: age, profession, goals and a short description.
  • Your development data: self-assessment scores, coaching conversations, tasks, the feedback you write about them, AI analyses, and progress history.

How we use it

Only to run UnfoldAI for you: to personalise coaching and tasks, analyse your feedback, show your progress and reports, and keep you signed in. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

Google user data

When you sign in with Google, UnfoldAI requests only basic profile scopes (openid, email, profile). This data is used only to create and sign in to your account and to show your name and picture in the app. It is not shared with third parties, except as described under “AI processing” for coaching content you write yourself (your Google data is not sent there). UnfoldAI's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

AI processing

To generate coaching replies, tasks and analyses, the text of your coaching conversations and feedback, together with relevant profile and progress context, is sent to an AI provider (currently Groq). API keys stay on our server; the AI never receives your password.

Storage and security

Data is stored in UnfoldAI's database on its server. Sessions use secure, http-only cookies; passwords are hashed; every request is checked so that users can only access their own data.

Your choices

You can edit your profile at any time. You can delete your account in the app (Profile → Delete account), which permanently deletes your profile, skills, tasks, feedback and reports.
